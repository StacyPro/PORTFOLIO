"Great hair isn’t a trend pulled from Insta or TikTok - it’s pure architecture. Whether it's scissor-over-comb, precision clipper work, or a hot towel shave, every cut and colour is designed to feel personal, effortless, and wearable for your everyday life.

Behind the chair, my standards are uncompromising. Beyond the chair, I thrive in a strong team culture - sharing advanced technical skills, mentoring developing stylists, and building the kind of loyal clientele that keeps business thriving."
